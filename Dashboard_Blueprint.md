# Dashboard Blueprint

Dashboard title: Smart Grid Cybersecurity Command Center

## Page 1: Executive Overview

Purpose: Give leadership a quick view of grid cybersecurity health.

KPIs:

- Total incidents.
- Critical incidents.
- Average risk score.
- Open vulnerabilities.
- Mitigation completion rate.
- Average response time.
- Average recovery time.

Visuals:

- KPI cards.
- Monthly incident trend.
- Severity donut chart.
- Top 5 risk regions.
- Top 5 high-risk assets.

## Page 2: Risk and Vulnerability Trends

Purpose: Identify how cybersecurity risk changes over time.

Visuals:

- Incident count by month.
- Average risk score by month.
- Critical vulnerabilities over time.
- Risk score distribution.
- Downtime trend.

Filters:

- Date.
- Region.
- Asset type.
- Severity.
- Threat vector.

## Page 3: Asset and Region Risk

Purpose: Locate the most vulnerable grid areas and asset classes.

Visuals:

- Region heat map.
- Asset type risk ranking.
- Top high-risk assets table.
- Risk score vs downtime scatter plot.

## Page 4: Threat Vector Analysis

Purpose: Understand cyberattack patterns.

Visuals:

- Incidents by threat vector.
- Severity by threat vector.
- Attack vector by region.
- Average response time by threat type.

## Page 5: Anomaly Detection

Purpose: Surface suspicious activity and unusual risk patterns.

Visuals:

- Flagged anomalies table.
- Incident spike trend.
- Assets above risk threshold.
- Box plot or histogram of risk score/downtime.

## Page 6: Mitigation Performance

Purpose: Measure whether controls and response actions are working.

Visuals:

- Mitigation status breakdown.
- Closure time trend.
- Open vs closed vulnerabilities.
- SLA breach count.
- Repeat incidents after mitigation.

## Page 7: Forecasting

Purpose: Predict future cybersecurity workload or risk exposure.

Visuals:

- Forecasted monthly incidents.
- Forecasted average risk score.
- Confidence interval band.
- Critical vulnerability projection.

## Page 8: Cohort and Retention Analysis

Purpose: Track long-term vulnerability behaviour.

Visuals:

- Cohort matrix by first incident month.
- Repeat incident rate.
- Risk reduction after mitigation.
- Asset retention in high-risk state.
